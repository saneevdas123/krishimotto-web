The platform is the KrishiMotto Android app, the admin, warehouse and lender portals, and the services offered through them — including crop advisory, field services, crop storage, digitised receipts and credit applications.

Some services are carried out by independent warehouse partners, lending partners, agri-experts and other providers. We introduce the booking. They do the work under their own arrangements with you.

3. Using the services

What you can book depends on your location, season, mapped land and partner capacity. The list of services can change.

When you map a field, you confirm you have the right to farm it. The boundary is used to plan the service, such as the area of a spray or a scan. It is not a legal title record, and we do not decide land disputes.

Dates we show are targets. Weather, safety, rules or partner capacity can move them. Reports, maps and calendars are guidance. They are covered in section 7.

4. Payments

Payments are collected by our payment partner using UPI and other supported methods. Each payment has an order reference. We do not store your full payment credentials.

A booking is confirmed when the payment partner reports success. If payment is still pending, the booking is not confirmed. A service that has already been delivered is not refundable. Other cancellations are handled according to how far the service has progressed.

Storage charges are based on the weight recorded at intake, which may differ from the weight you declared.

5. Storage and receipts

When you store crop, you are booking space with a warehouse partner. On intake they record the declared weight and the weighed weight. The platform then creates a digitised receipt and a QR code for that lot.

You confirm the produce is yours, is not already pledged or disputed, and can lawfully be stored. If the weighed quantity differs from what you declared, the weighed quantity is the one on the receipt.

The warehouse partner is responsible for physical custody, condition and release of the produce, under their arrangement with you. Stock that has been pledged cannot be released until that pledge is cleared.

A digitised receipt is a record on this platform so warehouses and lenders can verify stored produce. It is not, by itself, a negotiable warehouse receipt or a financial instrument.

Receipt and pledge events may be recorded using an identifier and a cryptographic hash. Those entries are public and permanent. They do not contain your name or bank details. The network is operated by third parties, and we are not responsible for outages or changes to it. Sensor readings, where a bay has them, are information only. They are not a guarantee of storage conditions.

6. Credit

We are not a lender. KrishiMotto lets you pledge a digitised receipt and sends the application, with the identity, bank and collateral details you provide, to a lending partner you choose.

The lender decides whether to approve, change or refuse the facility. Nothing in the app is an offer of credit, and approval is not guaranteed. Interest, charges, security and recovery are set by your agreement with the lender, not by these Terms.

By pledging a receipt you allow us to share that receipt, its status and the details you submitted with the lender you selected, and you agree the stock stays restricted until the pledge is cleared.

This matters most for chemicals and aerial spraying. Decisions you take from anything the app suggests are your own.
